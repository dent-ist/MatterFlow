### #1295 (Title column 120 px)

Confirmed: the page is right. D-43a (owner-adjusted in #1232) sets the Title minimum at 120 px and the guarantee at 160 px only for the default column sets (Pod Work 162, Task Queue 186, My Work 322 at 1366). Columns a person adds may squeeze the Title to 120 and the frame then scrolls (LC-09). UF should **not** change `column-prefs.ts` to 200. D-43's "(spring, min 200)" is the rule for the record lists (D-44u: Task Types Name at least 200; Clients "Client Name" 200), and it is stale for task grids. Proposed wording (to be applied to `docs/ux/design-guidelines.md` by the branch that owns it; I do not push there):

- D-43: replace "name or title (spring, min 200)" by "name or title (the one spring; min 200 on the record lists, min 120 on the task grids, D-43a)".
- D-43a: add after "the Title keeps at least 160 px on every global task grid": "(only for the default columns; a person who adds columns can take it down to its 120 px minimum and the frame scrolls, LC-09)".

Tell UF: change the spec number (row 4) and close #1295 as "spec stale".
