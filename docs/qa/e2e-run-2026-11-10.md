## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

The suite finally executes end to end on CI, and it is red for two reasons. (a) Most failures are specs that were not kept in step with the redesigns of the last two weeks (Matter fields epic: 8 matter tabs instead of 7 and new default columns; Asset numbering; Lists epic; UI2 Title Case wording, date formats and dropdown style; Rename to LPT Hub; Landing pages; Reports). The numbers below are therefore a lower bound; a second run after the fixes will show failures that are hidden today. The two small jobs (branding spec, sensitive-matter specs) are fully green.
